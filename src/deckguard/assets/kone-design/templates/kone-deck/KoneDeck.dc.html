Connected services

What predictive maintenance changes

  • Equipment reports its own condition around the clock, so faults surface before passengers notice them.
  • Technicians arrive with the diagnosis already made, which cuts a second visit out of most repairs.
  • Building managers see availability and usage in one place:
    • Uptime and response times per unit
    • Peak-hour people flow across the site
  • Service plans adapt to how a building is actually used, not to a fixed calendar.
